## Overview

The oneAPI Meetup Series — a community interview series hosted at Intel, featuring conversations with leaders across the oneAPI and open source ecosystem. Bi-weekly meetups with industry speakers from Intel, Red Hat, Codeplay, and the broader community.

**Grew the meetup community from 0 to 200 members in about 6 months.** The series was later transferred to the UXL Foundation.
