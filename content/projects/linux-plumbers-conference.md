## Conference Overview

Founding organizer of the Linux Plumbers Conference — the first conference of its kind bringing together developers from the Linux kernel with user space projects as part of the "plumbing" of the Linux kernel. Now a flagship Linux Foundation conference.

### Key Responsibilities

- Part of the founding team (2008–2009)
- Built out the conference IT infrastructure to ensure the best attendee experience
- Invited keynote speakers
- Recognized specifically for outstanding work in contributing to the success of the conference

### Impact

- Founded a conference that has become a cornerstone of Linux kernel and ecosystem collaboration
- Now operated under the Linux Foundation
- Continues to bring kernel developers together with user space projects worldwide
