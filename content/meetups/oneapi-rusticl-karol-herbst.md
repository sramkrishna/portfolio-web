## About This Meetup

The oneAPI Meetup Series was a community interview program hosted at Intel, bringing together developers, maintainers, and industry leaders to discuss the open source and heterogeneous computing ecosystem. Each episode featured a conversation with a key figure from the community — from kernel developers to compiler engineers to CEO-level leadership.

This episode features **Karol Herbst** on **Rusticl** — the Rust-based OpenCL implementation for Mesa — discussing its role in the open source GPU driver ecosystem and the future of portable compute.

*The series was later transferred to the UXL Foundation.*
