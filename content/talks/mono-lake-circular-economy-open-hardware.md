## Talk Summary

The Mono Lake story — exploring how the circular economy enables open hardware. Drawing on the Mono Lake metaphor, the talk examines sustainable hardware models, refurbishment, and how circular approaches make open hardware ecosystems viable.

### Topics Discussed

- The circular economy and sustainable hardware
- Open hardware ecosystems and their economic viability
- Refurbishment and extended hardware lifecycles
- How circular models enable broader access to hardware

### Notes

From the professional career span — delivered during the ITRenew era, connecting sustainability with open hardware.
