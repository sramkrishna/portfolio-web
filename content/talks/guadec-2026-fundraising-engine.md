## Talk Summary

Building the fundraising engine for the GNOME Foundation — exploring how community-driven organizations can build sustainable funding through campaigns, donor relations, and community engagement.

### Topics Discussed

- Sustainable fundraising strategies for open source foundations
- Community engagement as a driver of donations
- Donor relations and long-term financial health
- The role of the board in organizational sustainability

### Notes

Delivered at GUADEC 2026, the GNOME project's annual conference.
